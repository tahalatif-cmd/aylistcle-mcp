# AY Listicle plugin for Claude

AY Listicle is a network where companies get featured in each other's best-of listicles. This plugin connects Claude to your AY Listicle account through the hosted AY Listicle MCP server, so you can manage listicles and deals by asking in plain words.

## What it does

- Search other members' listicles by topic, domain rating and open spots
- Check your credits (spendable, held and pending)
- Request editorial inclusion in a host's listicle
- Propose a free swap of spots with another member
- Answer requests waiting on you, and read your inbox
- Add your own listicle pages to your product

Anything that spends credits or answers another member returns a preview first (the action, the cost and your balance afterwards) and only runs after you confirm.

## Setup

1. Create an AY Listicle account at https://www.aylistcle.com (credits come with a plan).
2. Install the plugin. It connects to `https://www.aylistcle.com/api/mcp/mcp`.
3. Sign in with your AY Listicle account when Claude asks (OAuth). No API key is needed.

## Example prompts

- "What's waiting on me this week?"
- "Show me listicles in cold email with an open spot and DR above 50."
- "Request a spot in the second one. Show me the preview first."
